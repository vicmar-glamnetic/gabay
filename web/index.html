Three jobs

Is my payslip right?

Enter what your payslip says beside what the law gives. Get a line-by-line difference for SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG, withholding tax and net pay — with the common innocent explanations for each gap, so you can ask HR a precise question instead of a suspicious one.

What do I bring?

Twelve government transactions with a tickable checklist that saves as you gather documents over days. Plus the section nobody publishes: why people get turned away — the laminated birth certificate, the missing parental advice, the barangay clearance for the wrong address.

What am I owed?

Six statutory leaves, the senior and PWD discount rules including the VAT treatment retail gets wrong, contract red flags, PhilHealth benefits, and an OFW recruiter check. Every entitlement names the law it comes from.

Why it is different

Free calculators are everywhere. None of them show their work.

Every result in Gabay renders as an itemised ledger with the rule cited beside each line. An HR officer can defend the number. An employee can challenge a wrong deduction and point at the schedule it came from.

No bare numbers anywhere in the app. If a figure appears without a rule next to it, that is a bug.

  • Eight payroll calculators, all four BIR pay frequencies
  • Base amounts transcribed from the published BIR table, never derived
  • Every statutory figure lives in one file with a source and a verified date
  • Long-press any peso figure to copy it; share any ledger as formatted text

Privacy, in plain words

This app stores everything on your phone and sends nothing to a server.

There is no account, no login, no analytics and no tracking. Gabay never asks for your name, your email, your number or your salary. Everything you type stays on the device.

The whole dataset compiles into the app, so it works with no connection — which is usually exactly when you need it, standing in a government queue with one bar of signal. Nothing in the app shows a loading spinner, because nothing is ever fetched.

What it will not do. It will not tell you whether you have a case, predict an outcome, or characterise an employer's conduct. It names the statute and points you to where to ask. It carries no list of recruitment agencies, because a fabricated “licensed” result could cost someone their placement fee. It has no chatbot, because a model that can invent a contribution figure destroys the one thing this app is for.
